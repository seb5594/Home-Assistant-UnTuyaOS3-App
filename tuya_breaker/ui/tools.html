Lightleak · chip, AP and flash backup

Experimental. Lightleak setup writes target flash and can brick your device. It installs a Wi-Fi stager and erases/writes a payload sector before reading a 2 MiB dump. The dump contains secrets and payload changes. This mode does not install replacement firmware.

Generic BK7231T/BK7231N variants replace model-specific firmware profiles. They still do not guarantee exploit compatibility. Confirm the chip, remove the target from SmartLife using “Disconnect and wipe data”, then follow the numbered console prompts. No variant is tried automatically.

Install the CustomAP release image on your spare ESP with a compatible graphical flasher. Power it on and check for LightleakIdle. The app configures it through the isolated radio; no phone app is required. Reset the companion before every new attempt.

Private flash backups

Complete 2 MiB dumps only. Download and store them privately; they contain keys and configuration. They are post-exploit snapshots, not pristine backups. Up to 16 are retained. Deleting a backup cannot be undone.
